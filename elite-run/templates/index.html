01 — About

A club,
not a club.

Elite Run started as four friends meeting at 6 AM on a Tuesday. Today, it's a thousand-strong community across seven cities — and the rule hasn't changed: show up, run, take care of the person next to you.

Open
Every run is free to join. No signup fees, no member tiers, no gear gatekeeping.
Paced
Trained pacers for 5:00/km through 7:30/km on every group run.
Safe
Sweeper at the back, hydration on long runs, first-aid trained leads.
