First Edition — Elite Marathon '24

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

First Edition Photos

The First Edition marked the beginning of what would later evolve into the Elite Run club. Designed as more than just a competitive run, the event was created to encourage people to step away from routine inertia and reconnect with movement, discipline, and community. The 5K route consisted of two laps of 2.5 kilometres, with mini water stations and checkpoints placed across the course.

Season 1 — Club Sessions

FROM 6
TO 103.

On 22nd September 2024, Elite Run Club conducted its first official club session at Botanical Garden, Lucknow, with just 6 runners. What began as a small gathering gradually developed into a steadily growing community. By Week 15 on 18th May 2025, attendance had grown to 103 participants — a movement built one session at a time.

Second Edition — Elite 5K Run '25

THE RETURN OF A MOVEMENT

Second Edition Photos

The Second Edition brought together nearly 250 runners from across the city for a morning that combined endurance, community, and impact. What began as a single run in 2024 had now evolved into a recognised youth-led platform where fitness became a shared experience rather than an individual pursuit.
